The story behind the fix

Problem · approach · outcome
01

The problem

On Windows, the model cache scanner could flag healthy caches as corrupted and sometimes fail to list cached models.

02

The approach

I initially suggested a separate command to diagnose cache issues. After discussing it with the maintainer, I saw that fixing the scanner itself was the better solution.

03

The outcome

I updated the scanner to handle Windows-generated metadata correctly and added a regression test. The PR was reviewed, merged, and included in the release.

One small fix, a more reliable cache scan.
The change is part of Hugging Face Hub and helps keep healthy Windows caches from being mistaken for corrupted ones.
